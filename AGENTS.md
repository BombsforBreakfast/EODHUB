## Rental promotions and bundles

Add future bundles, discounts and one-off promotions as optional offers within the existing rental checkout. Do not replace a customer's normal checkout experience or route them into a separate promotion-only flow unless Micheal explicitly requests that change.

Preserve independent property selection, standard $10/month or $100/year per-listing options, and property management with Available, Pending and Occupied statuses. A promotion must not force annual billing or lock the customer into all of their properties. Display the offer, eligible properties, total price, term and renewal choice clearly; retain the standard checkout options alongside it. Use existing billing and account infrastructure, and preserve saved listings, photos, statuses and paid access.
